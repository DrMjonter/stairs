Drop Ogg Vorbis music here (one or more files):
garage_01.ogg
garage_02.ogg
garage_03.ogg

Tracks play in order, skipping missing files, then repeat. Use actual Ogg Vorbis,
not MP3 renamed to .ogg. Stereo is supported. Music streams from disk and follows
the Minecraft Music and Master volume sliders. Four-second entry crossfade and
exit fade-out. No audio is bundled. Rebuild/restart after adding source files.
For resource-pack replacements, use assets/mjontersstairs/sounds/music/garage/.
Unused slots can produce missing-sound warnings during resource loading; they
are skipped during playback. See docs/parking-garage.md for details.
