# Echo Basin — destination 3

The test terrain preset uses custom Minecraft 1.21.1 density functions for broad,
terraced basins and organic, tapered stalagmites/stalactites. Their footprint
widens near the floor/ceiling and narrows toward the chamber middle, with small
3D-noise irregularities; the strongest formations can still meet as columns.
Two subtractive 3D-noise erosion layers cut broad grooves and smaller chips into
the formations. The noise grid uses four-block vertical cells to retain detail.
Dark Echo Stone replaces terrain in matching formation-noise bands, including
the formations and surrounding deposits at their bases. Bedrock rules run first.
On upward-facing surfaces above the fluid level, grass climbs roughly 4-7 blocks
above the local terrace, fading through a four-block randomized edge. Higher
formation ledges remain rocky; downward-facing stalactites do not receive grass.
Basin density and surface rules use the same unit horizontal noise scale so
the grass transition tracks each terrace elevation rather than a global Y cutoff.
The block uses textures/block/dark_echo_stone.png on all faces, Echo Stone sounds,
deepslate mining properties, self drops, and the Echo Basin creative section.
The authored darker texture is required; it is not generated by the mod.
Low-frequency basin noise selects floor terraces near Y=22/36/50/64/78, joined
by smooth cubic ramps rather than abrupt elevation jumps. Small floor variation
is reduced from 2.5 to 1.2 blocks and spread over wider areas. General blended
roughness is halved outside dark-stone noise bands; the formation erosion layers
and their strengths are unchanged. Low basins flood below Y=32;
upper shelves retain Echo Grass. Ceiling noise places the cavern ceiling near Y=108,
and a restrained blended-noise detail layer roughens walls and connections.
The fixed biome and Echo Stone terrain are retained. Bedrock seals the floor at Y=0 and roof
at Y=127. Echo Lava replaces the default molten pools below Y=32 in new chunks.
No vanilla Nether structures, vegetation, or natural biome mob spawns are included.
Coordinates are 1:1 with the Overworld.

## Listening Wells: first progression pass

One Listening Well design generates on dry cavern shelves, with a 1-in-48 chunk
attempt (four times rarer than the previous preset); unsuitable sites are skipped atomically.
They are 11 blocks wide, with a 24-block dark-stone shaft, descending lanterns,
four lit pillars and a brick/polished/chiseled platform. There are no ladders or
resonators. The three-by-three opening contains invisible well-barrier blocks:
they block players and mobs, but dropped item entities pass straight through.
Generation stays within its owning chunk and rejects bedrock, fluids, containers,
other structures, and previously built wells. No additional artwork is required.

Drop a Resonance Shard into an unused well. Crossing the invisible grate consumes
exactly one shard and reserves the well while prompting the thrower to speak.
Prompts, in order, are "I am still here.", "I can hear you.", "This is my voice."
After audible speech, the recording echoes back from three blocks inside the
shaft. Only when the echo finishes is the next reference granted: Presence,
then Response, then Recognition. Only shards actually thrown by a living player in Echo Basin
count; unowned machine/mob drops do not credit nearby players. The offering must
physically enter a complete well opening. A whole dropped stack consumes only
one shard; the rest continues down the shaft. Stay within eight blocks of the
well during capture/playback. The window to begin speech is 30 seconds; recordings
need at least half a second of audible audio, are capped at eight seconds, and
end after about 0.8 seconds of silence. Words are prompted, not speech-recognized.
The echo begins after another 0.75 seconds and uses the existing high-pass/reverb
DSP with a 2.5-second tail. Pending wells cannot be claimed by another offering.

Each well can be activated only once globally, by any player. Spent well centers
are persisted in Echo Basin StaircaseData; player references remain in Overworld
StaircaseData. Used grates light up and emit sparse particles, and cannot activate
again even for another player. Further offerings are not consumed by activation,
but still fall down the shaft. A player with all references cannot spend another
unused well. Barriers are unbreakable/immovable fixtures with no item or loot.
Sound/particles confirm activation; messages are localized. Calibration encounters
and the Listener are deferred. Connected Simple Voice Chat proximity voice is
required; private groups are never recorded. An unavailable voice connection
returns the offered stack. Timeout, leaving, death, disconnection, and normal
server shutdown cancel the reservation and refund one shard (inventory or rim
drop). The well remains unused, with no voice saved. The mod still loads without
Voice Chat; /stairs return remains an operator bypass, not a voiced completion.

Only audio during an offered, explicitly prompted well interaction is captured.
The prompt discloses that the line is stored on the server. After successful echo
playback, raw audio is atomically saved as 48 kHz, 16-bit mono WAV under the world
folder's mjontersstairs/voice_references/<player UUID>/. Random filenames avoid
overwriting recordings. Overworld StaircaseData stores each reference's relative
file path in EchoVoiceLines for future doppelganger playback. No boss behavior is
implemented yet. Recordings persist with the save; server owners should treat
them as player audio, include them in backups, and can remove them deliberately.
Automated tests use synthetic audio; actual microphone/network playback needs
a connected client playtest. Server capture/playback uses the official
[microphone packet event](https://voicechat.modrepo.de/de/maxhenkel/voicechat/api/events/MicrophonePacketEvent.html)
and [audio player API](https://voicechat.modrepo.de/de/maxhenkel/voicechat/api/audiochannel/AudioPlayer.html).

Until all three are restored, ascending the arrival stairs reports the missing
reference count. After completion, ascend them to return to the same Overworld
staircase, using the existing transfer animation. /stairs return is still an
operator testing bypass. This completion is not yet wired into global Mirror
World eligibility; the multi-dimension progression system remains future work.

Restart Minecraft and use fresh chunks to see natural wells. For testing on a
clear dry cavern shelf in Echo Basin (operator commands):

    /place feature mjontersstairs:listening_well
    /give @s mjontersstairs:resonance_shard 3

Move to separate chunks between place commands. The feature centers on each
chunk and scans Y=34-100 for a suitable shelf; vanilla reports placement failure
when none is available. Wells are terrain features, not /locate structures.
The previous resonator variants/resources were removed; there is no old-world
migration for this unshipped mod. Use newly placed/generated wells for testing.

Terrain tuning is data-driven in worldgen/density_function/echo_basin/ and
worldgen/noise/echo_basin_*.json. floor_height controls terrace levels, pillars
controls column threshold/width, and ceiling controls chamber headroom. This
does not add structures, mobs, waterfalls, or new blocks. Existing chunks are
unchanged; restart the server/client after updating and use fresh terrain or a
new test world. World-generation appearance still needs a client playtest.

Resonant Ore generates in Echo Stone: 16 vein attempts per chunk, size 8, Y=8–119.
An iron-tier pickaxe is required. Normal mining drops the custom Resonance Shard;
Fortune increases drops, and Silk Touch returns the ore block.
All materials appear in the Echo Basin creative-tab section.

Echo Lantern is a glowstone-like full cube using echo_lantern.png on all faces.
It emits light level 15, can be mined by hand, and drops one Echo Lantern without
Silk Touch. It is available in the Echo Basin creative section; natural generation
and crafting are not added yet. The IterationT patch maps it as glowstone-like.
Custom mono OGG one-shots belong in sounds/echo_basin/echo_lantern/: break_01.ogg
through break_04.ogg, hit_01.ogg through hit_04.ogg, place_01.ogg through
place_04.ogg, step_01.ogg through step_04.ogg, and fall.ogg. These sound events
remain silent until the files are supplied. Suggested lengths: hit/step 0.1-0.35
seconds; break/place/fall 0.2-0.7 seconds. The existing dimension echo applies.

Echo Stone uses echo_stone.png. Echo Grass uses echo_grass_top.png and
echo_grass_side.png, with Echo Stone on the bottom. The authored purple colors
are not biome-tinted. Nether-style surface rules place this stable, non-spreading
grass layer on dry cavern floors at Y=32 and above; ceilings remain Echo Stone.
The block has nylium mining and sound properties but does not decay in darkness.
Existing chunks are unchanged; explore newly generated terrain to see the layer.
Resonant Ore uses the supplied resonance_ore.png texture. The custom Resonance
Shard (mjontersstairs:resonance_shard) uses textures/item/resonance_shard.png.

Echo Stone building materials include plain stairs/slabs, Echo Stone Bricks with
stairs/slabs, Polished Echo Stone with stairs/slabs, and Chiseled Echo Stone.
All use Echo Stone sounds, pickaxe mining, self drops (two slabs for a double
slab), and the Echo Basin creative section. Crafting and stonecutting recipes
are included. Four Echo Stone make four polished blocks; four polished blocks
make four bricks; two stacked brick slabs make one chiseled block.
New textures in assets/mjontersstairs/textures/block are echo_stone_bricks.png,
polished_echo_stone.png, and chiseled_echo_stone.png. Each is a seamless square
texture used on all faces; stairs/slabs reuse their base material's texture.
These new textures are intentionally awaiting the author's artwork.

When Sound Physics Remastered is installed, Echo Basin applies a separate,
strong reflective profile to in-world sounds. Late reflection gains are
0.55/0.75/0.90/1.00, preserving 90% of the original direct gain. The profile
fades in and out over 30 ticks and restores the original environment on exit.
Music, UI, and auxiliary voice-chat sources remain unaffected.

Test with /stairs spawn echo in the Overworld, then climb the staircase.
Arrival retains the original tread-relative position and cuts stairs into dry
cavern ground below the roof. Obstructed or fluid-filled arrivals abort safely.
/stairs return returns to the recorded Overworld entrance.

Echo mechanics, listening structures, progression, and ARG exports are not implemented yet.

## Echo Lava and atmosphere

Echo Lava is a registered custom source/flowing fluid, liquid block, FluidType, and
bucket. It inherits lava spreading and water-contact solidification and emits light
level 15. It is harmless: no contact damage, ignition, or drowning; it extinguishes
existing fire. Lava's random fire-spreading ticks are disabled.
The fluid uses blue flame/tear particles instead of orange lava droplets.

Place textures in assets/mjontersstairs/textures/block:

- echo_lava_still.png: vanilla-style 16-pixel-wide animation strip.
- echo_lava_flow.png: vanilla-style 32-pixel-wide animation strip.

Frames are square, stacked vertically; higher-resolution equivalents also work.
Animation metadata is provided, at 2 ticks/frame still and 3 ticks/frame flowing.
Textures receive an opaque blue tint (ARGB FF669DFF); grayscale textures preserve
their detail best. Already-blue images are multiplied by that tint too.
Until images exist, animated water sprites are used as blue, opaque placeholders.
Resource reload (F3+T) picks up newly supplied textures.

Vanilla uses a custom skyless DimensionSpecialEffects with blue/violet biome fog
and a cool lightmap. Patched IterationT explicitly maps dimension_3 to its roofed
world-1 pipeline. A per-frame stairsEchoBasin uniform overrides Nether fog,
ambient illumination, and block-light warmth only while in Echo Basin. Echo Lava
is mapped to the lava emissive material. The actual Nether and other dimensions
retain their original shader settings. Run python tools/patch_iteration.py to
rebuild IterationT 3.2.0 - Stairs.zip; the original pack remains untouched.

Atmosphere changes apply immediately after restarting/reloading the dimension
settings. Existing generated lava blocks are not converted; visit new chunks.

## Custom sounds

### Simple Voice Chat

Optional integration targets Voice Chat API 2.6.20, compatible with the installed
NeoForge 1.21.1 Voice Chat 2.6.22. Both the speaker and listeners running Stairs
and Simple Voice Chat get four local voice echoes: original voice plus four repeats.
At the default delay they arrive at +1, +2, +3, and +4 seconds, at 30%, 15%, 7.5%,
and 3.75% PCM amplitude. The /basinecho toggle/delay settings also control voice.
Blacklist voicechat:voice to exclude all voice echoes, or voicechat:microphone /
voicechat:proximity to exclude self/received echoes independently.

Each phrase captures the speaker's initial eye position and facing direction
(including pitch, as observed on the client). Echoes travel down that direction:
repeat sources are 6, 12, 18, and 24 blocks from the initial position.
One second without audio starts a new phrase/anchor/direction. Walking or turning
afterward does not redirect an existing phrase. Hearing range is extended by 24
blocks for the displaced sources, but only clients that received the original
voice create echoes. Each client echoes only proximity voice it already
receives, plus its own transmitted microphone audio. Private/static group chat
is excluded, and the effect is disabled while participating in a group.
The original voice is not modified. Ordinary ambient voice echoes send no extra
voice data and do not write to disk; bounded temporary PCM queues are discarded
after playback. Offered Listening Well sessions are the explicitly disclosed
exception described above.
Pausing, leaving Echo Basin, disconnecting, or changing settings drops pending voice frames.
Already submitted Voice Chat buffers may finish their very short playback tail.
Generated echoes are locational channels and never enter the entity-voice capture
hook, preventing recursive voice echoing.

Integration uses the official [client audio event](https://voicechat.modrepo.de/de/maxhenkel/voicechat/api/events/ClientSoundEvent.html)
and [positional client channel](https://voicechat.modrepo.de/de/maxhenkel/voicechat/api/audiochannel/ClientLocationalAudioChannel.html)
APIs. Actual microphone/remote voice playback requires an in-game test.

Only echoed PCM gets the 300 Hz high-pass (low cut), four damped feedback delay
lines and two diffusion stages. The mix is 20% dry / 80% wet, with 0.88 reverb
feedback and a soft peak limiter. Each phrase/repeat has its own persistent DSP
state. Silence frames continue the tail for 2.5 seconds after speech stops;
tails also survive a subsequent phrase beginning at a new location.
Original microphone/received voice stays untouched. DSP is independent of Sound Physics.

### Regular sounds

Every sound actually started in Echo Basin gets one additional playback by default,
including music and UI sounds. Default delay is 20 client ticks (one second), with
30% of the original channel volume, the same resolved audio variant/pitch, and the
original sound position. This is independent of the Sound Physics reverb.
Looping originals get one extra, non-looping playback of their clip, not another
permanent loop. Replays never spawn further replays. Pausing freezes the delay.

Client settings are saved in config/mjontersstairs-client.toml under [echoBasin]:
enabled = true, delaySeconds = 1.0, blacklist = [].
Delay accepts 0–60 seconds, rounded to 50 ms client ticks; zero keeps the replay
but removes the intentional delay. Changing settings clears pending/current echoes.
Exiting the dimension, disconnecting, or reloading audio also clears them.

Client-only commands, no operator permission required:

- /basinecho: show settings.
- /basinecho on, off, or toggle: enable/disable replay.
- /basinecho delay 0.5: change delay; use 1 for the default or 0 for immediate playback.
- /basinecho blacklist add minecraft:ui.*: exclude matching event IDs.
- /basinecho blacklist add mjontersstairs:echo_basin/echo_lava/*: exclude matching audio assets.
- /basinecho blacklist remove minecraft:ui.*: remove an exclusion.
- /basinecho blacklist clear: clear all exclusions.

Blacklist entries match complete namespaced sound IDs or clip asset IDs; * matches
any sequence of characters. Entries affect only the replay, never the original.
Unstarted/muted/invalid sounds do not echo. Pending queues are bounded to 4096 for safety.

Place mono Ogg Vorbis clips (preferably 48 kHz) in assets/mjontersstairs/sounds/echo_basin/.
Keep recordings dry: Sound Physics adds the cavern echoes.
Break, hit, and place each choose randomly from four equally weighted variants.
Stone footsteps also choose randomly from four equally weighted variants; grass footsteps remain unchanged.
The old unnumbered break/hit/place filenames are no longer used.

- echo_stone/: break_01.ogg through break_04.ogg, hit_01.ogg through hit_04.ogg,
  place_01.ogg through place_04.ogg, step_01.ogg through step_04.ogg, fall.ogg.
  Resonant Ore shares these sounds.
- echo_grass/: break_01.ogg through break_04.ogg, hit_01.ogg through hit_04.ogg,
  place_01.ogg through place_04.ogg, fall.ogg. Grass footsteps retain vanilla NYLIUM_STEP.
- echo_lava/: ambient.ogg, pop.ogg, fill.ogg, empty.ogg.

Hit/step: 0.1–0.35 seconds; break/place/fall: 0.2–0.7 seconds.
Lava pop: 0.2–0.8 seconds; bucket fill/empty: 0.5–1.2 seconds.
Lava ambient: a self-contained 2–4 second murmur, not a seamless loop.
All are one-shots. Lava ambiance/pop play at 0.75/0.8 pitch, sounding lower and longer.
Missing files are silent until supplied; F3+T reloads added sound assets.

The IterationT uniform stairsEchoSubmerged is based on the camera position relative
to the custom source/flowing surface, independent of vanilla lava detection or dimension.
It selects blue submerged fog; ordinary lava keeps its orange fog.
Visual shader verification still requires running the client.

The IterationT test patch adds cloudy Echo Basin fog to the Nether composite
pipeline (which normally has distance fog, not the Overworld volumetric pass).
It marches 12 samples through drifting world-space 3D cloud noise, with baseline
density 0.0055 plus up to 0.030 in cloud pockets, bounded to 128 blocks (roughly
one third lighter than the initial cloudy preset). Fog
attenuates scene color and adds blue/violet scattering. It runs only when the
Echo Basin uniform is active and the camera is not submerged. Other dimensions
retain their original rendering. Echo Basin distance-fog density is also raised
from 0.010 to 0.018 for reflections and the non-volumetric underwater path.
Reload/select IterationT 3.2.0 - Stairs.zip after rebuilding the patch. Fog does
not require fresh chunks; the erosion and grass transitions do. Shader compiler,
appearance, and performance still require a live client playtest.
